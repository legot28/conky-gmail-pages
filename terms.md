# Terms of Use — Conky Gmail Desktop

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Conky Gmail Desktop is a personal utility intended for use by its owner on a Linux computer.

The application uses Google's Gmail API to retrieve unread message counts after the account owner has authorized access through Google OAuth 2.0.

The application is provided free of charge and without warranty. The owner is responsible for securing their computer, OAuth credentials, and Google account.

Use of the Gmail API remains subject to Google's applicable terms and policies.

For questions about this application, contact the owner through the Google account associated with its OAuth configuration.
