# Privacy Policy — Conky Gmail Desktop

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Conky Gmail Desktop is a personal Linux utility used by its owner to display the number of unread messages in a Gmail account.

## Information accessed

The application accesses the Gmail API through Google OAuth 2.0 to retrieve unread message counts.

## Use of information

Information retrieved from Gmail is used solely to display the unread message count on the owner's desktop.

## Sharing

The application does not sell personal information or share it with third parties for advertising.

## Security

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and should not be published or shared.

## Contact

For questions about this application, contact the owner through the Google account associated with its OAuth configuration.
